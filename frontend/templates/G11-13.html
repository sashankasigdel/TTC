Concept and Types of Industry

Industry is a group of manufacturers or businesses that produces a particular kind of goods or services by the use of factors of production. An industry is considered as a group of similar businesses. It is an organized form that produces goods or services for sale.

Classification of Industry

Based on Fixed Assets

  • Family-based operations
  • Local market focus
Cottage Industry
  • Traditional skills-based
  • Handicraft production
  • Home-based operations
Small Scale Industries
  • Limited capital and workforce
  • Semi-mechanized processes
  • Regional market reach
Medium-scale Industries
  • Moderate investment
  • Mechanized production
  • National market coverage
Large Scale Industries
  • Heavy capital investment
  • Fully automated processes
  • International market presence

History of Industrial Development in Nepal

Industrial Evolution: Many traditional cottage and small scale industries related to crafts, woods, and metals were established from the very beginning of civilization in Nepal.

Key Milestones: The history of organized industrial development dates back to 1936 AD during the reign of Rana Prime Minister Juddha Shamsher Rana, known as the father of industry in Nepal.

1936
First Industrial Venture

Biratnagar Jute Mill - First joint venture with Indian entrepreneurs marking the beginning of organized industry

1951
Post-Rana Initiatives

Government Planning - Formulation of various industrial plans and policies after Rana regime ended

1956
First Development Plan

Industrialization Milestone - Establishment of favorable policies and public enterprises with foreign assistance

1990
Economic Liberalization

Privatization Era - Enactment of Economic Liberalization Policy and privatization of public enterprises

2020
Contemporary Development

Modern Industrial Policy - Industrial Enterprise Act 2020 and new industrial estates in multiple districts

5

7

Enhances international relations and exposure
